## Details

- Inspired by a fellow researhcer and the T-shirt you recieve, the researcher, being pretty new to the field at this time, started trying to hack it.


- Via some communication, he got his hands on a pre-compiled list of Dutch Government subdomains.

- He had no idea what to do with it, so he started learning about Recon from pentester.land


- After that he was able to come back and find many vulnerabilities, most of which were flagged but one got selected as having **High** severity.
